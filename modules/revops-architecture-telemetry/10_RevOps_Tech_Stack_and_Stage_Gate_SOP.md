# RevOps Tech Stack & Stage Gate SOP

## Pipeline Stage Architecture
1. **Stage 1: Discovery & Risk Qualification** (Criteria: Baseline manual review rate identified)
2. **Stage 2: Technical & Data Trial Scope** (Criteria: Data partner network requirements mapped)
3. **Stage 3: Information Security & Compliance Audit** (Criteria: Vendor risk assessment cleared)
4. **Stage 4: Executive Sign-off & Contracting** (Criteria: Quantified ROI business case presented to CRO/CFO)
5. **Stage 5: Closed Won / Onboarding Setup** (Criteria: Contract executed, SDK sandbox active)
