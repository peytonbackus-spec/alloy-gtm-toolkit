# Fintech Signal Prospecting & Intent Trigger Catalog

## Core Intent Signals
* **Executive Hiring:** New VP of Fraud, Chief Risk Officer, or Head of AML onboarded within last 90 days.
* **Regulatory Compliance Actions:** Public CFPB audits, consent orders, or updated KYC requirement notices.
* **Product Expansion:** Launching new credit card, BNPL, or business banking products requiring KYB verification.
* **Legacy Vendor Friction:** High latency or downtime incidents reported across legacy bureau APIs.
