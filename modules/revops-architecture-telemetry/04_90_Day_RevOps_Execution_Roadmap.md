# 90-Day RevOps Strategy Roadmap for Alloy

## Days 1–30: Audit & Telemetry Alignment
* Audit Salesforce data hygiene, conversion bottlenecks, and opportunity velocity between Banks and Fintechs.
* Map current Clay enrichment workflows against key buyer titles (CRO, VP Fraud, Chief Compliance Officer).

## Days 31–60: Partner Ecosystem & Co-Sell Automation
* Operationalize co-sell attribution for Alloy's 270+ open data partner network within Salesforce.
* Build automated alert signals in Slack/Outreach when accounts trigger fraud executive hiring or regulatory audit flags.

## Days 61–90: Enablement & ROI Standardisation
* Deploy programmatic ROI calculators to enable AEs to build quantified CFO business cases early in sales cycles.
* Refine pipeline forecasting models based on historical stage-by-stage conversion velocity.
