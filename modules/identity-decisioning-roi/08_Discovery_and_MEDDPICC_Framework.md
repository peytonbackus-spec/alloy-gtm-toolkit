# Alloy Enterprise Discovery & MEDDPICC Framework

## Core Buyer Alignment
* **Economic Buyer:** Chief Risk Officer (CRO) or Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
* **Technical Buyer:** VP Engineering / Head of Fraud Operations
* **User Buyer:** AML & KYC Compliance Managers

## MEDDPICC Qualification Matrix
* **Metrics:** Baseline manual review rate (target: drop from >15% to <5%), synthetic fraud loss (bps), cost per manual review ($12–$15/review).
* **Economic Decision Criteria:** CAC Payback under 6 months, SOC2 Type II compliance, zero downtime API SLAs.
* **Decision Process:** Procurement $\rightarrow$ InfoSec Audit $\rightarrow$ Data Trial Validation $\rightarrow$ Executive Approval.
* **Identify Pain:** High labor costs tied to manual document verification; slow user onboarding causing dropped funnel conversion.
