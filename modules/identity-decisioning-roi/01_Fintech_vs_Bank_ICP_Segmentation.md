# ICP Segmentation & Tiering Model: Banks vs. Fintechs vs. Credit Unions

## Segment Matrix & Buying Drivers

| Criteria | Tier 1 Banks ($10B+ AUM) | Regional Banks ($1B–$10B AUM) | High-Growth Fintechs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Executive** | Chief Risk Officer / EVP Compliance | VP Operations & Fraud | Head of Product / Fraud Lead |
| **Core Pain Point** | Legacy point solution bloat, slow SARs | High manual review labor costs | Account takeover (ATO) & onboarding friction |
| **Alloy Value Prop** | 270+ open data partner orchestration | Automated KYC/KYB & AML workflows | Single API integration for fast decisioning |
| **RevOps Sales Motion** | Field-led, security audit, 6-9 mo | High-touch hybrid, 3-6 mo | Product-led / Signal-led outbound, 1-3 mo |
